How it works

We built our process so you never have to chase paperwork, prescriptions, or insurance approvals. Here's exactly what happens, start to finish.

1

Apply online or by phone

Fill out the short application on this site, or call (800) 704-6515. We'll ask about your diabetes care, your feet, and your insurance. It takes a few minutes.

2

We verify & coordinate

Our team contacts Medicare or your insurance carrier to confirm your therapeutic shoe benefit, then sends the prescription and certification forms to your physician to sign.

3

Professional fitting

A trained podiatrist, certified pedorthist, or licensed shoe fitter measures your feet and helps you choose the right style, size, and width. A professional fitting is required for coverage.

4

Your shoes arrive

Your shoes and custom-molded inserts ship to your door. On January 1st your benefit renews — and we'll remind you when it's time for your next pair.

Your annual benefit

What Medicare & most insurance plans cover

1 pair of therapeutic shoes

Extra-depth diabetic shoes in athletic, casual, or dress styles — professionally fitted to your feet, in widths up to 6E.

3 pairs of custom inserts

Custom or heat-molded insoles shaped to your feet that distribute pressure evenly — the key to preventing ulcers.

Renews every calendar year

Your benefit resets on January 1st. We track it for you and send a friendly reminder when you're eligible for your next pair.

Who qualifies? You must be diagnosed with diabetes, be treated under a comprehensive diabetes care plan, and have at least one qualifying foot condition — neuropathy, poor circulation, foot deformity, calluses at risk of ulcerating, current or previous ulcers, or partial/complete foot amputation.

Don't qualify for coverage? You can still protect your feet. Our self-pay program offers therapeutic shoes with specialty inserts starting around $99 — far less than typical specialist-office pricing.
