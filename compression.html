Compression garments

Compression can support lymphedema management by helping reduce swelling and improve mobility. We offer products designed to improve comfort and day-to-day management — billed through your plan when covered.

Compression wraps

Adjustable wraps from Sun Scientific and AMERX provide targeted pressure to support lymphatic drainage and reduce swelling — easy to put on, adjust, and wear all day.

Sleeves, stockings & fitted garments

mediUSA sleeves, stockings, and other fitted compression apparel for day and night use — properly sized to support circulation and comfort.

Compression matters. Choosing the right garments makes lymphedema easier to manage — contact our team for guidance in selecting appropriate compression products, or browse compression in the Quantum store.
