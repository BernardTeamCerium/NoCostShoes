Why physicians and podiatrists refer to No Cost Shoes

We prepare the prescription, statement of certifying physician, and all Medicare-required paperwork — your office just reviews and signs.

Referring a patient takes minutes

1

Send the referral

Call us at (800) 704-6515 or have your patient submit our online application with your practice information.

2

Sign the paperwork

We fax or send the prescription and certification forms to your office, pre-filled and ready for signature.

3

We take it from there
