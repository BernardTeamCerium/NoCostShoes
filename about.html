Our story

Founded by a Board-Certified Pedorthist

Quantum Medical Supply is more than a footwear and CGM provider — we are your dedicated partner in managing diabetes. With a deep commitment to your well-being, our team makes obtaining diabetic shoes and CGMs as straightforward and cost-effective as possible.

We work directly with Medicare and other healthcare plans to determine coverage eligibility for diabetic shoes and CGMs. Founded by a Board-Certified Pedorthist, our mission is to educate clients and connect them with qualified healthcare professionals to receive properly fitted diabetic shoes and integrated CGMs.

That means we verify coverage, prepare the physician paperwork, provide professional fittings — free in-home fittings available — bill insurance directly, and remind you when your benefit renews. You never chase a form or a fax.
