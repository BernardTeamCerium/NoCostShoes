No Cost Shoes exists for one reason: too many people who qualify for therapeutic footwear never get it, because the process feels complicated. We fixed the process.

Our story

Founded by a Board-Certified Pedorthist

No Cost Shoes was created by Quantum Medical Supply, a Medicare-approved supplier based in West Palm Beach, Florida, to educate patients and providers about diabetic footwear and the coverage Medicare provides.

Our founder, a Board-Certified Pedorthist, saw the same thing every week: patients with diabetes walking on feet at real risk — who had no idea their insurance would cover the exact shoes that could protect them. The benefit existed; the path to it was just too confusing.

So we built a service that removes every obstacle: we verify coverage, prepare the physician paperwork, provide professional fittings, bill insurance directly, and remind you when your benefit renews. You never chase a form or a fax.
