Our story

About Us

Quantum Medical Supply is more than just a footwear and CGM provider; we are your dedicated partner in managing diabetes. With a deep commitment to your well-being, our team is here to make the process of obtaining diabetic shoes and CGMs as straightforward and cost-effective as possible.

Quantum Medical Supply works directly with Medicare and other healthcare plans to determine coverage eligibility for diabetic shoes and CGMs. Our mission is to educate clients and connect them with qualified healthcare professionals to receive properly fitted diabetic shoes and integrated CGMs.

What we stand for

Our Philosophies

Commitment to Health Equipment Knowledge

We understand the health benefits and medical safety associated with properly fitted shoes and integrated CGMs, so we provide you with the knowledge you need to make informed decisions.

Empowering Clients Through Education

We strive to educate clients about utilizing appropriate medical devices and avoiding unnecessary complications.

Proactive Care and Financial Prudence

Educating and connecting our clients with leading healthcare professionals keeps them proactively managing their diabetes. We further benefit clients by preventing future expenses that result from improper medical device use.

Building a Unified Care Network

Streamlined Access to Diabetic Equipment
