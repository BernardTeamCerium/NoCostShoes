Continuous glucose monitors

CGMs are wearable devices that track your glucose around the clock and provide real-time insights — no constant finger sticks. We verify coverage and coordinate the prescription with your doctor.

Dexcom G6 & G7

Glucose readings on a compatible smart device or receiver, alerts for high or low readings, and data-sharing features that keep family and caregivers in the loop. Coverage depends on your insurance plan and medical eligibility.

FreeStyle Libre 2 Plus & 3 Plus

Ongoing glucose tracking with customizable alerts. The Libre 3 Plus is a small, discreet sensor that sends readings straight to a compatible smartphone.

Find the ideal CGM for your diabetes needs: contact our team for help understanding available systems and determining which option may be appropriate based on your prescription, device compatibility, and coverage.
